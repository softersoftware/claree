# Story refinement

Previous: [version planning](step_5_version_planning.md).

Before a story is implemented, it is refined. A UI designer polishes its part of the prototype into its mock-up, which shows its final interface, and an architect or a coder adds to the story its plan of implementation.

Next: once a story has its final interface and its plan, it is [implemented](step_7_implementation.md).

## Risks

Drifting from what was validated: a final interface that changes what the domain experts tried in the prototype, or a plan that builds more than the story asks.

## Glossary

- **Story refinement**: making a story ready to be implemented, with its final interface and its plan of implementation.
- **UI designer**: the person with the expertise to design the final look of an interface.
- **Architect**: the person with the expertise to decide how the digital tool is built as a whole.
- **Coder**: the person who writes the code of the digital tool.
- **Final interface**: the interface of a story as real people will see it, polished from the prototype.
- **Plan of implementation**: how a story will be built, written in the story before it is.
- **Mock-up**: the final interface of one story, as the domain experts can try it before it is real.
- **Demonstration**: the mock-ups of a version's stories together, connected to nothing outside them: already a first version of the digital tool.

## Rules

**Rule.** Every mock-up belongs to exactly one story, and starts from that story's part of the prototype.

**Rule.** A mock-up changes how the prototype looks, not what it does. What it does differently is a change to the prototype, tried again first.

**Rule.** A mock-up, and a demonstration, depend on nothing outside themselves. They can be shown at any moment, by anyone, with no consequence.
