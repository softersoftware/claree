# Feedback

Previous: the [release](step_8_release.md).

Real people use the digital tool, and say what is wrong with it or what they miss. Their feedback is gathered in one place, open to everyone, sorted by the makers, and answered. What it asks for goes back into the cycle as stories, or calls for new [workshops](step_1_workshops.md).

Next: a feedback that asks for more than a story calls for new [workshops](step_1_workshops.md); one that asks for a story goes to [story mapping](step_4_story_mapping.md).

## Risks

Feedback left unanswered: people stop giving it. And feedback taken as a specification: what someone asks for is a symptom of a need, not the story that answers it.

## Glossary

- **User**: a person who uses the digital tool. They are not a participant in the project, and need no account to give feedback.
- **Feedback**: what a user says about the digital tool, in their own words, at one time.
- **Problem report**: a feedback saying the digital tool does not do what it should.
- **Wish**: a feedback asking for something the digital tool does not do yet.
- **Feedback form**: where a user gives feedback, from the digital tool itself.
- **Following a feedback**: a user reading, at any time, what became of the feedback they gave, from where they gave it.
- **Sorting feedback**: deciding, for each new feedback, what it asks for and what becomes of it.
- **Answer**: what the project tells a user about their feedback.
- **Outcome of a feedback**: one of *already known*, *already planned*, *taken into account*, *fixed in a release*, *not planned*.

## Rules

**Rule.** Anyone using the digital tool can give feedback, without saying who they are.

**Rule.** Every feedback can be read by anyone, with what became of it.

**Rule.** A feedback holds nothing that says who gave it. A user who wants to be told what became of it follows it.

**Rule.** Every feedback is sorted. Sorting links it to the stories that answer it, existing or new, and gives it an outcome.

**Rule.** A feedback is never a story. The stories that answer it are written in the words of the domains, with their own business value and effort.

**Rule.** A feedback is answered, where everyone can read it, when it is sorted, and again when a release carries a story that answers it.

**Rule.** Every feedback names the version of the digital tool it was given from.
