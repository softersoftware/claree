# Workshops

The business is met in workshops: the informal side of the business, what was said, shown or recorded, as it came. The [domains](domains.md) are written from it.

## Glossary

- **Workshop**: one working session and what came out of it.
- **Date of a workshop**: the day it was held.
- **Title of a workshop**: what it was about.
- **Document**: one thing a workshop left behind. It says what kind it is — slides, a video, a recording, notes, a report, a transcript — and where it is, so that anyone reading the project can go back to it.
- **Text of a document**: what it says, when it is written directly in the project — notes, a report — and is then part of the project.

## Rules

**Rule.** A workshop is dated. It is not a source of truth: it is what was said on one day. What was understood later is a later workshop, or it is written in a domain, where the domain expert can contradict it.

**Rule.** A document can be added to a workshop after the day it was held — a report or a transcript often comes later — and it is always about that day.

**Rule.** Workshops are read by date, the most recent first.
