# Deployment

Previous: the [implementation](step_7_implementation.md).

Done stories are deployed: put in front of real people. A milestone may be deployed in parts, as its stories are done; it is reached once all its stories have gone out.

Next: real use brings feedback, which may call for new [workshops](step_1_workshops.md).

## Risks

Introducing bugs in front of real people. A deployment is fully tested before it goes out, and makers stay available to step in when it does.

## Glossary

- **Deployment**: putting done stories in front of real people, at one time.
- **The deployment that carried a story**: the one it went out in.
- **A milestone reached**: one whose stories have all gone out.

## Rules

**Rule.** Only a validated [demonstration](step_6_refinement.md) is connected to the outside world — where information is kept, how messages are sent — to become what real people use.

**Rule.** A deployment carries only done stories. Work in progress waits for the next one.

**Rule.** A story that has gone out names the deployment that carried it. Asking when something reached real people is asking about a story, not about a log.

**Rule.** Once the digital tool is in real use, a change still starts as a change to the business. Every new feature, and every new domain, goes through the same steps — glossary, rules, prototype, mock-ups, then a deployment. The order is the same on day one and in year five.
