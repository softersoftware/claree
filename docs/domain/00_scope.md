# Scope

A project starts with its scope. It is short, broad and deliberately vague, and it hardly changes over the life of the application. The precision lives in the [domains](02_domains.md), not here.

Next: once the scope is written, the domain experts explain the business in [workshops](01_workshops.md).

## Glossary

- **Scope**: a few sentences saying what the application is for and what it is not.

## Rules

**Rule.** The features of a project are drawn from its scope and from its domains. A feature the scope cannot account for is either a change of scope, said out loud, or not wanted.
