# Milestone planning

Previous: [story mapping](step_4_story_mapping.md).

The story map is sliced into milestones, and the stories to do are put in order. They are grouped into coherent milestones, each a set of stories that together make a step worth reaching. Within a milestone, they are prioritised by several criteria: the ratio of value to effort, the availability of makers, the availability of what they depend on, the highest risk first.

Milestones are prioritised too, mainly by the ratio of their total value to their total effort. Makers implement the stories in the planned order when they can, above all in the order of the milestones.

Next: the stories are [refined](step_6_refinement.md), then implemented, in that order.

## Risks

Milestones are meant to be small increments. A milestone with too few stories is not really usable; one with too many delays the feedback that comes with reaching it.

## Glossary

- **Milestone**: a coherent set of stories, planned to reach real people, which can be released in parts as its stories are done.
- **Blocked by**: the stories a story needs done before it can be done, because it needs what they bring.
- **To do**, **in progress**, **done**, **in production**: where a milestone is, in that order, from where its stories are.

## Rules

**Rule.** A milestone is to do while all its stories are to do, done once each of them is done or in production, in production once all of them are in production, and in progress in between.
