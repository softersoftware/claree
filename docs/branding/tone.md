# Tone

How Supersoft speaks, in its interface and in its own documents.

**Sober, plain, direct.** A label names a thing. It never sells it.

- Say the plain word: *Projects — 2*, not *Found for you — 2*.
- Nothing is addressed flatteringly to the reader: no *for you*, no *your journey*, no congratulation when something is done.
- No marketing verbs — discover, unlock, empower, boost. Supersoft finds, opens, keeps, asks.
- No exclamation marks, and no sentence whose job is to say the product is good.
- Say what is missing as plainly as what is there: *Nothing found.*
- A project's own words are its own. Supersoft never rewrites them, and never translates them.

If a sentence can be cut without losing a fact, cut it.
