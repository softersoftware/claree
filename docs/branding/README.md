# Branding

How Clarée presents itself. What a branding holds, when it is chosen and who agrees to it is in [the branding domain](../domain/branding.md).

Direction chosen: slate, the second of three [branding prototypes](https://softersoftware.github.io/claree/branding/002/). Not agreed yet.

- **Personality**: clear, attentive, sober. Made for the domain experts first, charities above all; never an app generator ([workshop](../workshops/20261011%20branding/notes.md)).
- **Name**: Clarée.
- **Tagline**: Making digital tools with the people who need them. In French: « Construire des outils numériques avec celles et ceux qui en ont besoin. »
- **Tone**: [`tone.md`](tone.md).
- **Colours**: cool greys, one deep blue. Their values are in [`theme.css`](theme.css).
  - *paper*: the background of every page.
  - *card*: what stands on the paper: the header, a project, a message.
  - *ink*: the text.
  - *muted*: what is said about something rather than the thing itself: a scope, a section's title, who is signed in.
  - *rule*: borders and separations.
  - *accent*: what can be done: a link, a button, the focus. Also the bar above the header and the logo's square. *accent-strong* when pointed at.
  - *warn*: what went wrong, or could not be done.
- **Typography**: IBM Plex Serif for what is read, a project's name, its scope, a page's title; IBM Plex Sans for everything else. Section titles in small spaced capitals.
- **Logo**: a square in the accent, beside the name in spaced capitals ([`logo.svg`](logo.svg)). The icon of the application and of a browser tab is the square alone, light on the accent ([`icon.svg`](icon.svg)).
- **Iconography**: none yet. When one is needed: outlines, square ends, no fill, the colour of the text around them.
- **Shapes and density**: nearly square corners; borders, never shadows. One column, wide enough to read a scope in one look, with room between sections.
